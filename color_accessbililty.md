# Color Contrast in Accessibility

## What is Color Contrast?
Color contrast refers to the difference in brightness between text and its background.

## Why It Matters
Poor contrast makes content hard to read, especially for users with visual impairments.

## Real-World Example
Light grey text on a white background often fails accessibility standards.

## Best Practices
- Follow WCAG contrast ratios
- Use tools like contrast checkers
- Avoid color-only indicators

## Common Mistakes
- Relying only on brand colors
- Ignoring dark mode contrast

## Key Takeaway
Good contrast improves readability and inclusivity.
